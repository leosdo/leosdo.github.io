In collaboration with Hexagem AB, I correlate **structural heterogeneity with emission deficiencies in InGaN nanoLEDs**.

The approach combines scanning nano-diffraction with complementary X-ray mapping techniques at NanoMAX: X-ray excited optical luminescence (XEOL), X-ray beam-induced current (XBIC) and X-ray fluorescence (XRF). I built nano-resolved XEOL fitting pipelines to connect local strain and composition to optical performance.
