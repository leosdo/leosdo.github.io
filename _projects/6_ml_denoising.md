Noise in diffraction microscopy images biases the centre-of-mass evaluation used to calculate local strain and tilt. I built a **machine-learning denoising workflow** that makes this evaluation more accurate.

The workflow **won the Machine Learning for Microscopy Hackathon 2** (2026).
