At NanoMAX, users have limited beamtime and need to make decisions fast. I develop tools that help them get the best possible data:

- **Real-time processing of scanning-diffraction data**, so users can decide on the fly during beamtime.
- An **online toolbox for experiment feasibility**: crystallography, absorption and elemental excitation calculations, free for users.
- Feasibility evaluation of sample specifications and safety together with users before beamtime, and installation of sample environments and detectors.

**Code:** [Nanomax-tools](https://github.com/leosdo/Nanomax-tools) on GitHub, tools for experiment planning at the NanoMAX beamline.
