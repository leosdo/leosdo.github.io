How do ferroelectric domains respond when mechanical and electrical stimuli act together? With ETH Zurich and Chalmers, I resolve nanoscale ferroelectric domain dynamics under **electrical bias and nano-indentation**, linking these stimuli to local switching behaviour and phase transitions.

A correlative in situ nano-XRD protocol follows indentation-induced plastic deformation with electric bias, mapping whether phase transitions are reversible. This work on pressure-nucleated and field-erased BiFeO<sub>3</sub> domains was presented at XTOP 2026.

Earlier, using in situ X-ray diffraction under electric-field cycling, I showed how compositional disorder governs the non-polar/polar phase boundary in BiFeO<sub>3</sub>–SrTiO<sub>3</sub> relaxor ferroelectrics.
