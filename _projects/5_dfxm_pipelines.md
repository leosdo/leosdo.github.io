Dark-field X-ray microscopy (DFXM) produces large image stacks that need careful processing before they become strain and tilt maps. I develop Python pipelines covering:

- image **registration** and **denoising**
- **Q-space transformation**
- **strain and tilt** calculation

**Code:** [DFXM-superstructure](https://github.com/leosdo/DFXM-superstructure) on GitHub.
