Strain and tilt in ferroelectric HZO

In situ nano-diffraction of Hf₀.₅Zr₀.₅O₂ device stacks under electrical bias

Hf0.5Zr0.5O2 (HZO) is a CMOS-compatible ferroelectric for memory and electromechanical devices. Together with DTU, I investigate the origin of electrostriction and giant pseudo-piezoelectricity in HZO device stacks.

Using in situ scanning nano-diffraction at NanoMAX under electrical bias, we showed that in epitaxial HZO thin films the electric field acts on the spatial heterogeneity of strain and tilt rather than on the average response, identifying tilting as a stabilizer of ferroelectricity.

Ongoing work uses nano resonant elastic scattering to map ferroelectric polarization underneath electrodes.

(Bergne et al., 2026; Bergne et al., 2026)
